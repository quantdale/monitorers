Predecessor candidate `ebea5b2` is committed/pushed on `agent/frontend-successor-2026-11-13`. Its full local qualification is recorded in the two preceding changes. This campaign was selected because native visual QA found a real hardware/data discrepancy, not for unrelated infrastructure polish.

## Reproduction and authority

- Native UI shows Intel plus two RTX 4050 cards. WMI `Win32_VideoController` reports Intel UHD and one NVIDIA RTX 4050 Laptop GPU.
- Independent read-only DXGI probe (`inspect-dxgi.py` / `dxgi-before.json` in the session folder) reports Intel `0x00000000_0x000183B8`, Nvidia `0x00000000_0x000186D3`, and Microsoft Basic Render Driver `0x00000000_0x0001865D` with SOFTWARE flag 2.
- WMI GPU engine counters expose all three LUIDs. Current `assign_captions_to_luids` sorts those IDs, zips them to two unrelated captions and gives the surplus the last Nvidia caption. Therefore Basic Render Driver receives an Nvidia name; Nvidia gets a duplicate suffix. NVML's deliberately unique-name reconciliation then cannot attach the physical Nvidia device's readings. The mismatch is directly executable on this host.
- Microsoft documents `DXGI_ADAPTER_DESC1.AdapterLuid` as the adapter identity and `DXGI_ADAPTER_FLAG_SOFTWARE` as software classification: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/dxgi/ns-dxgi-dxgi_adapter_desc1 and https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/dxgi/ne-dxgi-dxgi_adapter_flag . Local windows-rs binding source will supply exact signatures.
